4. Evidence Interpretation

Passing JUnit/JDBC and packaged-JAR SQLite tests establishes the tested application paths only. It does
not establish graphical usability, teamwork authenticity, remote GitHub CI, or submission completion. The
release remains partially verified until the GUI, extraction/build packaging, and real team/remote require-
ments are completed.
